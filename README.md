# TC Framework

Password-protected interactive TC framework hosted with GitHub Pages.

The page and its assets are encrypted together with StatiCrypt 3.5.4. The editable source and password are not stored in this repository.

## Versions

- [Original framework](https://mattien1984.github.io/tc-framework/)
- [Fade In](https://mattien1984.github.io/tc-framework/fade-in/): seven cursor-controlled stages reveal the four center foundations individually, then the three complete outer rings. Upcoming layers stay at 8% opacity.
- [Zoom In](https://mattien1984.github.io/tc-framework/zoom-in/): the same seven stages build outward from You Create Time, with a smaller center and pure-white titles.
- [Live](https://mattien1984.github.io/tc-framework/live/): twenty-three keyboard-controlled scenes. All the artwork is visible from the start, labelled only You Create Time. The center is a clear Pie: the You Create Time circle with Time is Creation, All Time Matters and Time Creationist as three slices around it, with no fill so the background and the Fabric of Time show through. Each arrow-key press adds one label: the three center slices, then each ring's sections clockwise from the top followed by its level name in Title Case, and finally the Belief Horizon. Blocks not reached yet wait at 30% opacity, and the block for the current scene glows, starting with You Create Time.
- [Center Options](https://mattien1984.github.io/tc-framework/center-options/): the Live presentation with four treatments of the center to compare, each with roomier center rings (the framework's overall size is unchanged). Original is the approved center. Spectrum brings the five section colours into the center with a slow sweeping light, keeping the labels at the top. Pie keeps the You Create Time circle and turns the three phrases into slices of one ring around it, with a clear center; it is the version now used in Live. Pie Spectrum is the Pie coloured like Spectrum. Press 1–4 or use the buttons to switch; H hides the buttons. Add #original, #spectrum, #pie or #pie-spectrum to the address to open one directly.
- [Live 2](https://mattien1984.github.io/tc-framework/live-2/): a separate copy of Live with Rob's center: the see-through Section Tint fill, its colours shifted so no slice matches the section beside it, a You Create Time circle half as large again (its words the size of the section titles), and even gaps between the pieces instead of divider lines. Time Creationist sits at the top, Time is Creation lower right and All Time Matters lower left; they light up after the core in that order. Each level's arrow ring comes up as its band begins, and the middle and outer level names read Purposely Shaping Evolution and Perpetually Actualizing Potential. Presenting by hand, one more press of next on the last scene brings the closing glow. F (or the play button that appears when the mouse moves) plays the whole sequence, starting calm and speeding up to the end in about 8 seconds; as it lands, a soft, blurred light rises behind the whole framework into a corona beyond the horizon, then pulls back into the center and fades. Shift+F toggles full screen.
- [Live 2 Alt](https://mattien1984.github.io/tc-framework/live-2-alt/): the earlier Live 2 (before Rob's latest edits) with a neutral see-through fill with no colour on the three slices, rather than the Section Tint colours.
- [Pie Options](https://mattien1984.github.io/tc-framework/pie-options/): a separate copy of the Live presentation for trying fills on the clear Pie center, with every fill still see-through so the Fabric of Time shows. Clear is the current center; Level Glass tints each slice in its level's colour; Radiant turns You Create Time into a soft light source; Section Tint runs the five section colours lightly through the slices; Luminous Edges lights each piece from its edges inward. Press 1–5 or use the buttons to switch; H hides the buttons. Add #clear, #level-glass, #radiant, #section-tint or #luminous to the address to open one directly.

Fade In and Zoom In add a subtle breathing glow and a soft expanding, fading pulse every three seconds from the active center edge while holding steps 1–4.

## Updating

Edit the local working copy, rebuild the encrypted page using the local build script, then push the resulting deployment files. GitHub Pages deploys from the root of the main branch.
